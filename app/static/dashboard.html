Net Dealer Directional Profile

netDIR(P) = net delta of the open-interest book at price P, normalised by total OI. Positive (red) = book net-long upside → dealers net-short → downward pull. Negative (green) = dealers net-long → upward pull. "C" is where the curve crosses zero.
